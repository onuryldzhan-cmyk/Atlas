## Architecture decisions
- **Reuse Atlas's own archive reader.** `soak_report` uses it rather than parsing the archive itself, so the
  report cannot disagree with startup about what the archive holds.
- **No tuning knobs.** The pre-registered numbers are module constants, not options.
- **Archive completeness check.** It can only lower a verdict. It is the agent's addition after the owner's approval
  and needs the owner's confirmation before day 0 (plan §2 step 1).
